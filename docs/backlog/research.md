# Research Backlog

Research is a future product frame and must remain disabled until the study protocol, consent materials, privacy review, and institutional approvals are complete.

## Future research questions

- Which content categories are most strongly associated with changes in self-reported mood, stress, sleep, or wellbeing?
- How do exposure duration, repeated exposure, and user feedback relate to those outcomes?
- Do local, user-controlled insights change content choices or perceived wellbeing?
- How do model errors and content ambiguity affect participant trust?

## Required before implementation

- Study hypotheses and analysis plan
- IRB determination/approval
- Informed consent, parental permission, and child assent where applicable
- Participant safety and escalation plan
- Data retention, deletion, breach, and access procedures
- De-identification and re-identification risk assessment
- Separate research-export consent and revocation flow
- Pilot protocol with predefined quality and stopping criteria

## Deferred self-report instrument

If a study needs self-reported mood, stress, sleep, or wellbeing, design it as a study-specific instrument after protocol and ethics review. It is not a desktop-product feature and must not be inferred from browsing data.
